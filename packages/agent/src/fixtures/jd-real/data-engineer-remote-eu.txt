Data Engineer (Analytics Platform), Remote, EU

At Northwind Freight we move about 2 million parcels a month for online shops in 12 countries. Our data team is small (four people) and the business runs on it: pricing, carrier routing and every finance report come from our warehouse.

The job
You'll build and run the pipelines that feed the warehouse, own our dbt project, and make the data our analysts and pricing team depend on reliable and fast.

Requirements
- 3+ years as a data engineer
- Strong SQL and Python
- Production experience with dbt and a cloud warehouse (we use Snowflake)
- Orchestration with Airflow, Dagster or similar
- You care about data quality: tests, monitoring, alerting

Bonus points
- Kafka or another streaming tool
- Terraform
- Logistics or e-commerce background

Fully remote within EU time zones (CET ±2). €65k–80k depending on experience. Contractor or employee.
